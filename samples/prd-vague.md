# App idea — FixIt

**From:** Danny Okafor  
**To:** dev team  
**Date:** July 2026

---

Hi team,

So the idea is basically an app like Uber but for handymen. You know when something breaks at home — a leaky tap, a door that won't close, a light fitting — and you have to spend ages finding someone reliable? That's the problem. People don't know who to call and they're scared of getting ripped off.

The app should make it super easy. You open it, describe what's broken, and someone comes to fix it. On the other side, tradespeople can sign up and get jobs through the app.

---

## The main thing

The core thing is matching people who need something fixed with people who can fix it. It has to be fast and it has to feel trustworthy. The ratings are really important for that — if someone has 50 five-star reviews you're going to trust them.

We need to make money somehow. Probably take a cut of each job. Percentage TBD, maybe 15–20%, we haven't decided. The tradesperson should see their earnings clearly.

---

## Other things

- Customers should be able to upload a photo of the problem
- There should be a chat between the customer and the tradesperson once they're matched
- Payments go through the app, not cash
- Both sides need accounts
- The customer should get a quote before they commit
- GPS so you know where the tradesperson is when they're coming
- Reviews and ratings obviously
- The tradesperson should be able to set what jobs they do and what area they cover

---

## What it should look like

Clean and simple, like the big apps. Not technical. My mum should be able to use it.

Mobile is the priority, most people will use their phones. But a web version would be nice too, for the tradespeople to manage their jobs on a laptop.

---

## Notes

We want to launch in about 4 months. Budget is limited, we need to keep it simple for v1.

I know this sounds like Uber for X which everyone says is overdone but I really think the home services market is still broken and there's a gap for something that actually works and has good UX.

Any questions just ask!

Danny
